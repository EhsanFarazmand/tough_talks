The Problem

We rehearse presentations.
Never conversations.

The most consequential moments of our lives — asking for a raise, ending a relationship, setting a boundary with a parent — arrive with zero preparation and zero support. We improvise through them, often badly, and carry the damage for years.

😶

We freeze or over-apologize

Under pressure, we default to our worst patterns — caving too early, over-explaining, or going silent when we need to speak.

🌀

We escalate accidentally

A tone shift, a poorly timed word, a defensive reflex — and a conversation that started well becomes a conflict neither person wanted.

📵

No safe place to practice

You can't rehearse with the person you're about to confront. Friends give biased advice. Therapists cost money. You go in blind.

The Solution

Two modes.
One conversation.

Tough Talks works in two distinct phases — before and during. Each mode uses Gemma 4's capabilities differently, building toward the same outcome: you walk into any tough conversation prepared, and you walk out having said what you meant.

◎ PRACTICE MODE

The Rehearsal Room

Before the conversation happens, Tough Talks builds a simulation of the other person and lets you fight, fail, and learn in private — as many times as you need.

  • 🧠 Persona Construction Describe the person, paste their messages, or record how they speak. Gemma builds a behavioral simulation of them — their objections, their tone, their triggers.
  • 🎭 Adversarial Roleplay The simulation argues back, deflects, guilt-trips, or goes silent — whatever this person realistically does. Not a cooperative AI. A resistant one.
  • 📋 Post-Round Debrief After each practice, Gemma marks exactly where you lost ground, over-apologized, or missed an opening — with the line you should have used instead.
  • 🔁 Scenario Branching Explore different openings. What if you start soft? What if you lead with data? Gemma forks the simulation so you can test multiple strategies before committing.
● LIVE MODE

The Conversation Co-Pilot

During the real conversation, Tough Talks listens silently via earpiece or glanceable screen — detecting what's happening emotionally and surfacing guidance in real time, invisibly.

  • 👁 Emotion Radar Live audio analysis tracks both speakers' emotional states — tension, frustration, openness — and displays a real-time heat map on your screen.
  • 💬 The Whisper Subtle on-screen prompts appear at critical moments: "They just made a concession — acknowledge it before responding." You see it. They don't.
  • ⚠️ Escalation Alert Detects when the conversation is about to go off the rails — pace, word choice, silence patterns — and suggests a specific de-escalation move.
  • 📝 Live Transcript + Bookmarks Auto-transcribes in real time. You can silently bookmark any moment mid-conversation to revisit in the debrief — without breaking your presence.

Why Edge-First

The conversations you'd
never cloud-upload.

Tough Talks runs entirely on-device via Gemma 4. This isn't just a technical choice — it's the only ethical architecture for an app handling the most private moments of people's lives.

Zero data exposure

Your divorce conversation, your mental health disclosure, your salary demand — none of it touches a server. Not anonymized. Not encrypted on a cloud. Simply never sent.

Works anywhere conversations happen

A parking lot before a confrontation. A hospital room. A flight. A remote village. Tough conversations don't wait for good WiFi. Gemma on-device doesn't either.

Persistent personal memory

Your Talk DNA, person profiles, and outcome history accumulate locally over months. No account, no sync, no breach risk. Your data lives where you live.

E2B / E4B for always-on listening

Gemma's smallest models handle live audio monitoring at minimal battery cost. The larger model activates only for debrief generation — intelligent resource management built in.
